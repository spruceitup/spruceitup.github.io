About

Spruce It Up Reporting Sync is an internal tool built and used only by Spruce It Up Garden Centre Inc. (the "Company"). It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone outside the Company.

The tool reads data from the Company's own business systems, such as its accounting and point-of-sale systems, and copies it to the Company's internal reporting database for business reports and dashboards.

Terms of Use

  • The tool may be used only by the Company and its authorized staff, and only with the Company's own accounts.
  • The tool reads data only. It does not create, change or delete anything in the connected systems.
  • The tool is for the Company's internal business use and is not available to other businesses or the public.
  • The Company may change or stop using the tool at any time.

Privacy

Data accessed: business records in the Company's own connected systems, such as customers, sales, invoices, payments, products and services, vendors and bills.

Use: internal reports and dashboards for the Company only.

Storage: on systems controlled by the Company. Access is limited to authorized Company staff.

Sharing: data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Credentials: connection credentials issued by each connected system are stored securely on Company systems and are not shared outside the Company.

Retention: data is kept as long as the Company needs it for reporting. After a system is disconnected, no new data is collected from it.

Disconnecting

An administrator of a connected system can disconnect the tool at any time from that system's app or integration settings. Once disconnected, the tool can no longer read data from that system.

If you arrived here after disconnecting the tool, the disconnection is complete and no further action is needed.
