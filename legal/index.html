About

Spruce It Up Reporting Sync is an internal tool built and used only by Spruce It Up Garden Centre Inc. (the "Company"). It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone outside the Company.

The tool reads the Company's own QuickBooks Online data and copies it to the Company's internal reporting database for business reports and dashboards.

Terms of Use

  • The tool may be used only by the Company and its authorized staff, and only with the Company's own QuickBooks Online account.
  • The tool reads data only. It does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks Online.
  • The tool is for the Company's internal business use and is not available to other businesses or the public.
  • The Company may change or stop using the tool at any time.

Privacy

Data accessed: accounting records in the Company's QuickBooks Online account, such as customers, invoices, payments, products and services, vendors and bills.

Use: internal reports and dashboards for the Company only.

Storage: on systems controlled by the Company. Access is limited to authorized Company staff.

Sharing: data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Credentials: connection credentials issued by Intuit are stored securely on Company systems and are not shared outside the Company.

Retention: data is kept as long as the Company needs it for reporting. After the tool is disconnected, no new data is collected.

Disconnecting

A QuickBooks Online administrator can disconnect the tool at any time from QuickBooks Online under Apps > My apps. Once disconnected, the tool can no longer read data from QuickBooks Online.

If you arrived here after disconnecting the tool, the disconnection is complete and no further action is needed.
